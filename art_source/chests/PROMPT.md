## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: production 2D game sprite sheet for AURA, a clean minimal ice skating game.
Create ONE wide sprite sheet containing exactly TWO views of the SAME small ice treasure chest, side by side, on a genuinely transparent alpha background. LEFT view: chest CLOSED. RIGHT view: the identical chest OPEN with its hinged lid lifted back. Both chest bodies must have exactly the same size, angle, ground baseline and horizontal placement within their respective equal-width cells. Leave generous clear transparent margins, including above the closed chest to match the open lid's canvas. No cell dividers or labels.
Design: simple elegant faceted frosted ice, light silver-grey and very pale cool blue; broad low-poly planes rather than lots of triangles. Thin restrained brushed-gold edge bands and one small geometric gold latch. Broad readable front face, a narrow right side visible, very mild view from above, like an orthographic 2D side-scrolling pickup. Solid crisp silhouette, flat bottom, understated crisp shading. It must still read as a chest when shrunk to about 32 pixels wide. On the opened version, a small warm gold light INSIDE the dark ice-lined cavity, with no rays or exterior glow (the game adds those).
Style: clean, minimal, polished low-poly ice; NOT cozy, not cute, no wood, no medieval ornament, no realistic photograph, no black outlines, no snow, no scenery, no floor, no cast shadow, no particles, no sparkles outside the chest, no diffuse bloom, no text, no watermark, no gradients fading into a painted checkerboard. Transparent pixels around both isolated objects. Keep gold a small accent and ice the dominant material.
